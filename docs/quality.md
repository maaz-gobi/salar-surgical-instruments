# SalarSurgicals — Quality & Certifications

Quality is the foundation of medical instrument manufacturing. Here is how SalarSurgicals approaches it.

## Certifications

### ISO 13485
Our quality management system is certified to ISO 13485 — the international standard for medical device quality management.

### CE Marked
Our instruments carry CE marking, indicating conformity with applicable European health and safety standards.

### FDA Registered
SalarSurgicals is FDA registered.

## Materials

All instruments are manufactured from premium surgical-grade stainless steel, selected for corrosion resistance, durability, and suitability for repeated sterilization.

## Manufacturing Heritage

SalarSurgicals is based in **Sialkot, Pakistan** — the world's leading center for surgical instrument craftsmanship, with a manufacturing tradition spanning generations. We have been manufacturing since 2005.

## Export Standards

With buyers in 45+ countries, our instruments and processes are aligned with international medical-device expectations, and we are experienced in export documentation and logistics.
